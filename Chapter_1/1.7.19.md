# Exercise

Do all the books you own total at least one million pages? How many total pages are stored in your school library?

# Solution

No, I'm not own too much to reach 1 million pages.

About school library, I can estimate about ~3600000 pages

First, I estimate in a book there is 300-600 pages.

Then, the school's bookshelf can filled up to 50 books in 1 side.

Times it with 2 we have the pages of 1 bookshelf.

There are about 20 bookshelf in 1st floor in library. So times it with 20 we have the number of pages for the 1st floor.

There are 3 floors in my school so we multiply it for 3 then we have the answer.
