Reconsider the pirate problem above, where only one indivisible dollar is to be
divided. Who gets the dollar and how many are killed?

# Solution

Same with 2-51 but only $1 to divide.

With 2 pirates: P5 takes \$1, P6 gets \$0
With 3 pirates: P4 gives \$1 to P6

- P4=\$0, P5=\$0, P6=\$1

With 4 pirates: P3 gives \$1 to P5

- P3=\$0, P4=\$0, P5=\$1, P6=\$0

With 5 pirates: P2 gives $1 to P4 or P6

- P2=\$0, P3=\$0, P4=\$1 (or P6=\$1), P5=\$0

With 6 pirates: P1 gives $1 to P3 or P5

- P1=\$0, P2=\$0, P3=\$1 (or P5=\$1), others=\$0
