# Exercise

Six pirates must divide $300 dollars among themselves. The division is to proceed as follows. The senior pirate proposes a way to divide the money. Then the
pirates vote. If the senior pirate gets at least half the votes he wins, and that division remains. If he doesn’t, he is killed and then the next senior-most pirate gets
a chance to do the division. Now you have to tell what will happen and why (i.e.,
how many pirates survive and how the division is done)? All the pirates are intelligent and the ﬁrst priority is to stay alive and the next priority is to get as much
money as possible.

# Solution

We will sort six pirates from smallest to largest.

So the most senior pirate is Pirate 6.

And the least senior pirate is Pirate 1.

With 1 pirate(P6): Take all the money.

With 2 pirates(P6,P5):
- P5 make a pirate proposes.
- Pirate 5 vote yes, Pirate 6 vote no
- P5 will get \$300, P6 \$0.

With 3 pirates (P4, P5, P6):

- P4 needs 2 votes (including his own)
- P6 knows if P4 dies, P6 gets \$0
- P4 proposes: P4=\$299, P5=\$0, P6=\$1
- P4 and P6 vote yes -> passes

With 4 pirates (P3, P4, P5, P6):

- P3 needs 2 votes total
- P5 gets \$0 in next round, so P3 gives P5=\$1
- P3 proposes: P3=\$299, P4=\$0, P5=\$1, P6=\$0
- P3 and P5 vote yes -> passes

With 5 pirates (P2, P3, P4, P5, P6):

- P2 needs 3 votes total
- P4 and P6 get \$0 in next round
- P2 proposes: P2=\$298, P3=\$0, P4=\$1, P5=\$0, P6=$1
- P2, P4, P6 vote yes -> passes

With 6 pirates (P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6):

- P1 needs 3 votes total
- P3, P5 get \$0 in next round
- P1 proposes: P1=\$298, P2=\$0, P3=\$1, P4=\$0, P5=\$1, P6=$0
- P1, P3, P5 vote yes -> passes
